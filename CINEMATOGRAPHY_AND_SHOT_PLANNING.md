## 1. Cinematographic Principles

Every shot generated by the studio is governed by physical cinematography rules rather than generic video prompts. The `CinematographyShotPlanner` decomposes scenes into an ordered list of `PlannedShot` records, assigning explicit lens parameters, optical characteristics, lighting keys, and camera motions.

---

## 2. Shot Scale & Lens Pairing Guidelines

The planner pairs canonical shot scales with realistic lens focal lengths to maintain natural perspective:

| Shot Scale | Canonical Lens | Depth of Field (DOF) | Intended Psychological Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EXTREME_WIDE / ESTABLISHING** | 18mm – 24mm | Deep focus (f/8 – f/11) | Grandeur, environmental isolation, spatial context |
| **WIDE** | 28mm – 35mm | Moderate deep focus (f/5.6) | Character within environment, spatial relationships |
| **MEDIUM** | 50mm | Standard natural perspective (f/2.8) | Human conversation, physical gestures, balanced focus |
| **MEDIUM_CLOSE_UP** | 85mm | Shallow portrait focus (f/2.0) | Emotional intensity, facial nuance, dialogue delivery |
| **CLOSE_UP** | 105mm | Very shallow focus (f/1.8) | Intimate emotion, dramatic focus, character revelation |
| **EXTREME_CLOSE_UP / INSERT**| 100mm Macro | Razor-thin focus (f/2.8 Macro) | Tactile textures, mechanical precision, narrative clue |

## 3. Camera Movement Taxonomy

Camera movement adheres to Part 03's canonical optical flow taxonomy:
- `STATIC`: Locked off on tripod for formal stability and dialogue.
- `PAN_LEFT / PAN_RIGHT`: Horizontal rotational sweep around camera axis.
- `TILT_UP / TILT_DOWN`: Vertical angular pivot revealing height or scale.
- `DOLLY_IN / DOLLY_OUT`: Physical movement along optical axis changing perspective.
- `TRACKING`: Parallel movement tracking subject motion.
- `HANDHELD_JITTER`: Controlled organic instability conveying documentary urgency.

---

## 4. Lighting Direction & Ratio

Each shot explicitly defines lighting setups:
- **Key Light:** Primary illumination angle (e.g. "Key light 45° camera left at 5600K daylight").
- **Fill / Ambient:** Shadow density control (e.g. "Low-contrast negative fill camera right").
- **Back / Rim Light:** Subject separation (e.g. "Strong 3200K tungsten rim accentuating shoulders").
- **Practical Lights:** In-scene motivated sources (e.g. "Fluorescent tubes", "neon signage").
