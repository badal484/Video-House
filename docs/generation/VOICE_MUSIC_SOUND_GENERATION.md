# Voice, Music & Sound Generation Integration
**Project:** Universal AI Video Production Studio  
**Department:** 15_ai_generation & 14_audio_production  
**Document:** VOICE_MUSIC_SOUND_GENERATION.md  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

---

## 1. Division of Responsibilities

- **Department 15 (AI Generation)** owns: Provider integration, model routing, prompt preparation, licensing clearance, and raw audio asset synthesis.
- **Department 14 (Audio Production)** owns: Multitrack timeline layout, clip editing, equal-power crossfading, DSP filtering, dynamic dialogue ducking, stem summing, and EBU R128 loudness mastering.

---

## 2. Integrated Audio Generation Modalities

1. **Text-to-Speech (TTS) & Dubbing**: Generates spoken dialogue lines from script scenes with character identity consistency, emotional inflection, and phoneme-level word markers.
2. **Text-to-Music**: Generates background musical scores and transition stagers matching the scene's emotional valence and tempo.
3. **Text-to-Sound (SFX)**: Synthesizes spot sound effects and atmospheric Foley synchronized to visual actions.
