## 2. Cryptographic Consent Records
Every voice talent profile is registered with a `BiometricConsentRecord`:
- `consent_id`: Cryptographic UUID.
- `talent_name`: Legal name of the voice actor or rights holder.
- `voice_id`: Profile identifier.
- `authorized_purposes`: List of approved productions or genre categories.
- `expiry_date`: ISO-8601 expiration date.
- `digital_signature`: SHA-256 signature validating the contractual consent manifest.

## 3. C2PA Content Provenance & Watermarking
All localized audiovisual exports generated by Department 17 attach C2PA-compliant metadata:
- **Audio Watermarking:** Inaudible spectral watermarks embedded into dubbed dialogue stems indicating AI generation.
- **Video Metadata Manifest:** C2PA JSON-LD manifest documenting:
  - Original footage hash.
  - ASR and Translation engine identifiers.
  - Neural voice model identifier.
  - Lip-sync alteration mask and toolchain metadata.
