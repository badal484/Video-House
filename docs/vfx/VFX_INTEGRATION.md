# VFX INTEGRATION WITH EDITING & CREATIVE DIRECTION
**Department:** 13_vfx_compositing  
**Topic:** Inter-departmental Protocols with Departments 04, 08, 09, and 21  

---

## 1. Integration with Department 04 (Creative Direction & AI Director)

- **Input:** When Department 04 produces a `ProductionBlueprint`, its `PlannedShot` items contain metadata:
  ```json
  {
    "shot_id": "shot-004",
    "required_vfx": true,
    "vfx_notes": "Replace TV screen with animated stock chart; clean up mic boom at top frame",
    "duration_seconds": 3.5
  }
  ```
- **Bridge Action:** `DirectorVFXBridge` queries planned shots requiring VFX, instantiates a `VFXShotTask` in `core/assets/database.py`, and triggers `AI VFX Supervisor` to generate the initial `CompositingGraph`.

---

## 2. Integration with Department 08 (Non-Linear Editing Engine)

- **Timeline Replacement:** When a VFX shot composite completes rendering:
  1. The output file is registered in the Central Asset Registry (`AssetRecord` with `AssetRole.GENERATED_VIDEO`).
  2. `TimelineVFXBridge` updates the target clip in the OpenTimelineIO timeline.
  3. The clip can be placed on track `V1` as a direct cut replacement, or on track `V2` as an alpha overlay.

---

## 3. Integration with Department 09 (Motion Graphics)

- Motion graphics generated by Department 09 (such as kinetic typography, lower thirds, or UI animations) output transparent PNG sequences or ProRes 4444 video.
- In Department 13, these assets are consumed directly by `MEDIA_INPUT` nodes and mapped onto tracked planes or merged with light wraps.

---

## 4. Integration with Department 21 (Storage & Asset Management)

- All rendered composites, tracking JSON caches, and alpha matte masks are registered in `core/assets/database.py` with unique SHA-256 checksums, enabling zero-duplication caching across re-renders.
